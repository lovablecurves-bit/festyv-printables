How to run Nativity trivia for every age

Split your night into rounds. Round one should be multiple choice so kids ages six and up can play without embarrassment. Round two can be fill-in-the-blank for teens and adults. Round three is true-or-false myth-busting, which always gets laughs. Keep score lightly; a candy cane or hot cocoa packet is enough of a prize.

Read Luke 2:1–20 aloud before you start. When someone misses a question, have the host read the matching verse from the answer key. That turns the game into a short Bible study without feeling like a lecture.

Easy nativity trivia questions for kids

  1. In which town was Jesus born? (Bethlehem)
  2. Who was Jesus’ mother? (Mary)
  3. Who was Mary’s husband? (Joseph)
  4. Where was baby Jesus laid? (In a manger)
  5. Who told the shepherds the good news? (An angel / the angel of the Lord)
  6. What did the wise men follow? (A star)
  7. Name one gift the wise men brought. (Gold, frankincense, or myrrh)
  8. What did the angels say? (“Glory to God in the highest…”)

Print these on half sheets so every child has a pencil copy. For a longer kids’ night, add picture bingo from a Christian Christmas party games printable pack.

Medium questions for the whole family

  1. Which prophet said a ruler would come out of Bethlehem? (Micah)
  2. Who ordered the census that brought Mary and Joseph to Bethlehem? (Caesar Augustus)
  3. What does “Emmanuel” mean? (God with us)
  4. Which Gospel tells about the shepherds? (Luke)
  5. Which Gospel tells about the wise men? (Matthew)
  6. Where did Joseph take Mary and Jesus to escape Herod? (Egypt)
  7. Who was the Roman ruler of Judea when Jesus was born? (Herod / Herod the Great)
  8. What town did Mary and Joseph live in before the census? (Nazareth)

Harder “Bible scholar” questions

  1. Who met the infant Jesus in the temple and was waiting for the consolation of Israel? (Simeon)
  2. Which prophetess also gave thanks in the temple? (Anna)
  3. On which day was Jesus circumcised and named? (The eighth day)
  4. What offering did Mary and Joseph bring according to the Law? (A pair of turtledoves or two young pigeons)
  5. Which Old Testament verse is linked to “Out of Egypt I called my son”? (Hosea 11:1)

True or false: common Nativity myths

  • The Bible says there were three wise men. (False — it names three gifts, not three men.)
  • The wise men arrived the night Jesus was born. (False — Matthew describes a house and a young child.)
  • The shepherds followed the star. (False — the star is in Matthew’s account of the wise men.)
  • An innkeeper is named in Luke. (False — Luke says there was no room in the inn, but no innkeeper is named.)
  • Angels praised God saying “Glory to God in the highest.” (True — Luke 2:14.)

Myth rounds are perfect after dessert when energy is high. Keep a KJV or preferred translation nearby for quick checks.

Printable vs homemade trivia

Homemade lists are free and personal. Printable packs win when you need consistent formatting, scorecards, a separate answer key, and enough questions for 30–50 guests. If you are hosting church or office parties, a polished PDF also looks intentional next to your cocoa bar and Christmas gift tags.

For a full evening, combine trivia with Would You Rather and bingo from the 25-game bundle, then close with one card from your Advent scripture cards for kids set.

Hosting tips that keep the peace

  • Form mixed-age teams so no child sits alone with a blank sheet.
  • Allow open-Bible mode for younger kids; run closed-Bible for the scholar round.
  • Time each round (8–10 minutes) so the night does not drag.
  • Celebrate effort: read the verse even when the team is wrong.
  • Send winners home with a small printable prize certificate or a scripture card.

Frequently asked questions

How many nativity trivia questions for family night is enough?

Twenty to thirty questions across easy, medium, and true/false rounds fills about 30–40 minutes. Add a tie-breaker if you have competitive teens.

What Bible translation should I use for answers?

Pick the translation your family or church already reads. Festyv’s Advent and many Christian printables use KJV wording; announce the translation before you start so nobody argues over phrasing.

Can I use these questions at church?

Yes for your own group. For a large event, a licensed printable like Festyv’s Christmas trivia game keeps everything neat with an answer key.

How do I include preschoolers?

Give them picture bingo or let them sit with a parent team and hold the pencil. Save fill-in-the-blank rounds for older kids.
