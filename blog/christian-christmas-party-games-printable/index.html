Why printable Christian Christmas games work so well

Printable games solve three December problems at once. First, you do not need craft supplies or Wi-Fi. Second, you can print extras for last-minute guests. Third, every player gets the same clear sheet, so shy kids and new church visitors can join without feeling lost.

Look for packs that include answer keys, half-page options to save paper, and a mix of seated and active games. A strong Christian Christmas party games printable bundle usually mixes trivia, bingo, Would You Rather icebreakers, left-right gift games, and story sequencing so you can fill a 45- to 90-minute gathering without repeating the same format.

Seven faith-friendly game formats that always land

1. Nativity story sequencing

Players put key events in order: Gabriel’s visit to Mary, the journey to Bethlehem, the manger, the shepherds, the wise men, and the flight to Egypt. Use it as a warm-up after a short reading from Luke 2. Teams of three finish faster and talk more.

2. Christmas Bible trivia

Split questions into easy (kids), medium (families), and hard (Bible scholars). Always print the answer key with verse references so the host can turn wrong answers into a 15-second teaching moment. Pair this with a dedicated nativity trivia questions for family night if trivia is your main event.

3. Nativity bingo

Picture bingo keeps preschoolers in the game while word bingo works for teens and adults. Call slowly, celebrate blackout winners with a small candy cane prize, and keep a spare calling sheet.

4. Would You Rather Christmas edition

Light icebreaker prompts (“carol in the snow or bake all day?”) get quiet rooms talking in under five minutes. Festyv’s Would You Rather Christmas game printable is built for kids and adults at the same table.

5. Left-right gift pass

Read a short Christmas story with the words “left” and “right” highlighted. Guests pass a wrapped gift each time. Faith versions use a Nativity-themed script so the story stays on Jesus instead of Santa lore alone.

6. Charades or Pictionary from the Christmas story

Prompts like “shepherds watching flocks,” “following the star,” and “presenting gifts” work for youth groups. Keep teams mixed by age so younger kids can act while older kids guess.

7. Secret Santa with a questionnaire

Office and small-group exchanges run smoother when everyone fills out a short form first. See our guide to a Secret Santa questionnaire for work, then print Festyv’s Secret Santa questionnaire.

A sample 75-minute church Christmas party timeline

  1. 0–10 minutes: Welcome, name tags, cocoa station, soft carols.
  2. 10–20 minutes: Would You Rather icebreaker in pairs.
  3. 20–40 minutes: Nativity trivia in teams of four (easy then medium rounds).
  4. 40–55 minutes: Bingo or left-right gift pass.
  5. 55–70 minutes: Advent scripture card reading and short prayer. Pair with Advent scripture cards for kids.
  6. 70–75 minutes: Photo, thank-yous, send-home gift tags.

Printing tips for sharp, low-ink pages

  • Print at 100% / Actual Size on US Letter or A4; do not “fit to page.”
  • Use draft mode for practice rounds and best quality for the final set.
  • Cardstock (65–80 lb) makes bingo and scripture cards last for years.
  • Laminate answer keys and calling cards if your church reuses them.
  • Store PDFs in a shared drive so next year’s volunteers are not starting from scratch.

How to choose a Christian Christmas party games printable pack

Ask four questions before you buy or download:

  1. Does it include Scripture references or a clear Nativity focus?
  2. Are there answer keys on separate pages?
  3. Can kids and adults play the same night without two totally different kits?
  4. Is the license clear for church and family use (not resale)?

Frequently asked questions

What should I include in a Christian Christmas party games printable pack?

Aim for at least one icebreaker, one trivia or sequencing game with an answer key, one active game (charades or left-right), and one quiet option like bingo. Festyv’s Christmas party games bundle covers 25 games so you can mix seated and active play.

Are printable Christian Christmas games okay for mixed ages?

Yes. Use picture bingo and easy trivia for kids, medium and hard rounds for teens and adults, and team play so grandparents and toddlers share a table.

Can churches print multiple copies?

Most personal-use digital downloads allow printing for your own church event or family. Do not resell or upload the PDFs. Check the listing license; Festyv listings are for personal, family, and church use, not commercial redistribution.

How many games do I need for a one-hour party?

Three to four games is plenty: one icebreaker, one main trivia or bingo block, and one closing gift or scripture activity. Over-planning leads to rushed transitions.
