Why work questionnaires should stay light

Family questionnaires can ask clothing sizes and wish-list links. Work versions should not. Stick to coffee orders, desk snacks, hobbies, scents (or no scents), stores for gift cards, and a clear “please avoid” line for allergies. That keeps the exchange friendly for new hires and cross-department teams who are not close friends yet.

The best Secret Santa questions for coworkers

  1. Name and team or department
  2. Coffee, tea, or neither—and how you take it
  3. A snack or candy you never turn down
  4. Sweet or savory?
  5. Favorite color for mugs, notebooks, or socks
  6. Candles and scents: yes, or no thanks?
  7. Something that would make your desk nicer
  8. A hobby you enjoy outside work
  9. Books, shows, music, or games you like
  10. A store or café where a gift card would get used
  11. Please avoid (allergies, dislikes, things you already have too many of)
  12. Gift ideas under our budget

Twelve questions is plenty. If you move the form online, keep it to eight so people finish it. Festyv’s Secret Santa questionnaire printable is designed as a coworker favorites survey for office and family exchanges—print, fill, draw.

How to run the exchange in seven steps

  1. Announce early. Two to three weeks before the party is ideal.
  2. Set one budget. Agree as a group; when in doubt, choose the lower middle amount.
  3. Hand out questionnaires with a deadline a few days before names are drawn.
  4. Draw names with folded slips. If someone draws themselves, redraw.
  5. Pass the matching questionnaire only to the person who drew that name.
  6. Shop and wrap with tags that name the recipient, not the giver.
  7. Exchange and reveal at the party—guesses first make it more fun.

Remote and hybrid teams

Ship gifts to home addresses collected privately by the organizer, or set a digital gift-card budget with the same questionnaire. Hold a short video reveal so people still get the fun of guessing. Digital printables help here: everyone downloads the same form, fills it, and emails it to the organizer.

Pair the questionnaire with party games

After gifts, keep energy up with a quick round of Christmas Would You Rather or trivia from the 25-game Christmas party bundle. Finish take-home treats with watercolor Christmas gift tags so even small desk snacks look thoughtful.

Hosting a faith-based nonprofit or church staff party? Mix in a few nativity trivia questions for family-style rounds that stay respectful for mixed beliefs at work—or keep games fully secular icebreakers if that fits your workplace better.

Budget ideas that still feel personal

  • $15–20: favorite candy variety pack, nice pen, sticker set, cocoa bombs
  • $25–30: coffee gift card + snack, desk plant, cozy socks in their color
  • $40–50: hardcover book in their genre, quality mug + beans, small headphones pouch

Always read the “please avoid” line. A perfect candle is a poor gift for someone who wrote “no scents.”

Organizer checklist

  • Participant list with email or desk location
  • Printed questionnaires plus extras
  • Name slips and a bowl
  • Budget reminder on every form
  • Party date, time, and reveal plan
  • Backup gift (unopened) in case someone forgets

Frequently asked questions

What is a Secret Santa questionnaire for work?

It is a one-page form each coworker fills out before names are drawn, covering drinks, snacks, hobbies, and things to avoid so gifts are personal without being awkward.

How many questions should an office form have?

Eight to twelve. More than that and people rush or skip lines. Festyv’s printable keeps the list work-friendly.

Can I use the same form for family?

Yes. For family you may add sizes and wish-list links. The Festyv Secret Santa questionnaire works for office and family gift exchanges.

What if someone forgets their gift?

Keep one neutral backup gift and a gift card within budget. The organizer can quietly step in so nobody is left out.
